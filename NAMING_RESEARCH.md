# Naming research

**Run 15 of [`EXECUTION_PLAN.md`](EXECUTION_PLAN.md)** · Research carried out 26 September 2026

> **This document is research, not legal clearance.** Nothing here is a trademark opinion,
> a freedom-to-operate opinion, or a guarantee that a name can be used. A qualified
> trademark attorney must clear the chosen name in every market before it is published.
> Availability signals recorded here are point-in-time and can change within hours.

---

## 1. Why the name has to change

`PRODUCT_PLAN_V2.md` and `DEBRIEF_FOR_OPUS.md` already record that **WriteRight** is
heavily used. The problems, restated:

| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| Descriptive and weak | "Write" + "right" is the plainest possible description of a writing corrector. Descriptive marks are hard to register and hard to defend. |
| Crowded | The compound is in wide use across writing tools, education products, and agencies. A web search for the exact phrase returns unrelated commercial uses in the same field. |
| Homophone collision | *WriteRight*, *Write Right*, *Wright*, *Rite* are indistinguishable in speech. A spoken recommendation does not reach the right listing. |
| Untranslatable pun | The wordplay has no Arabic equivalent. Half the product's audience gets a meaningless English compound. |
| No `.com` | `writeright.com` and the obvious variants are long gone. |

**Decision rule carried forward from the user's instruction:** do not publish publicly
under the WriteRight name until these conflicts are addressed.

## 2. Screening criteria

Every candidate was scored against nine criteria before any availability research.

| # | Criterion | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pronounceable by an English speaker on sight | Word of mouth is the main discovery channel for an extension |
| 2 | Pronounceable by an Arabic speaker on sight | Half the product's audience |
| 3 | Uses no phoneme Arabic lacks | Arabic has no **/p/** and no **/v/**; MSA has no hard **/g/** (Egyptian does). A name with them is mispronounced by default |
| 4 | No negative or embarrassing meaning in Arabic | Including near-homophones in Egyptian and Gulf dialects |
| 5 | No negative meaning in English | Including obvious playground distortions |
| 6 | Two syllables or fewer, ≤ 7 letters | Fits a 16-pixel badge and a Chrome Web Store title |
| 7 | Spellable after hearing it once | No silent letters, no ambiguous doubling |
| 8 | Not descriptive of the goods | A descriptive mark is weak; a suggestive or arbitrary one is defensible |
| 9 | Distinct from existing writing tools | Grammarly, Wordtune, QuillBot, ProWritingAid, Hemingway, Writer, Sudowrite, LanguageTool, Typely, Outwrite |

## 5. Shortlist of three

### 1. Saqel  ·  صَقْل

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /ˈsɑː.kel/ · AR *ṣaql* — two syllables, no /p/, /v/ or hard /g/ |
| **Meaning** | To polish, to burnish. In Arabic it is used of refining metal *and* of refining prose |
| **Fit** | The flagship mode is literally called **Polish**. The name and the product's core promise are the same word in two languages |
| **Arabic check** | No negative sense. No unfortunate near-homophone found. The root ص-ق-ل is unambiguous |
| **English check** | Reads cleanly. No distortion found |
| **Evidence for** | No product, company, store listing or extension found under this name |
| **Evidence against** | `saqel.com` is registered and parked for sale, so acquisition would cost money or the product uses `saqel.app` / `getsaqel.com`. GitHub username `@Saqel` is taken by an individual |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### 2. Tanqih  ·  تَنْقِيح

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /tænˈkiː/ or /tanˈkiːh/ · AR *tanqīḥ* |
| **Meaning** | Revision; the emendation of a text. It is the word an Arabic editor uses for the job |
| **Fit** | Names the whole product rather than one mode, which suits a five-mode tool better than Saqel does |
| **Arabic check** | Precise, professional, and slightly formal. No negative sense |
| **English check** | Spellable after hearing it, but the final /ḥ/ will be dropped by English speakers, giving "tan-KEE" |
| **Evidence for** | No software product, company, store listing or repository found. The only results are dictionary entries |
| **Evidence against** | Seven letters and the *qih* ending will be misspelled by English speakers, which hurts search discovery. `tanqih.com` is parked on Afternic |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### 3. Sabk  ·  سَبْك

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /sæbk/ · AR *sabk* — one syllable |
| **Meaning** | To cast or forge metal. In classical Arabic rhetoric, سبك is *the cohesion of style* — precisely what an editor works on, paired with حبك, coherence of meaning |
| **Fit** | The most intellectually satisfying of the three: it is the Arabic critical term for well-made prose |
| **Arabic check** | Recognised by educated Arabic speakers; the rhetorical sense is not common knowledge |
| **English check** | Short and memorable, but the final /bk/ cluster does not exist in English and will be said as "sab" or "sabik" |
| **Evidence for** | No writing product found under the name. `usesabk.com` is unregistered |
| **Evidence against** | **Phonetic near-match to "Sabak"**, a live Google Play app from a Kuwaiti logistics company. Different class and different spelling, but the collision is real in speech and in a Gulf market. `sabk.com` has been registered since 2003 |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### Recommendation

**Saqel** is the strongest of the three on the evidence gathered: the cleanest phonetics
in both languages, the tightest semantic fit with the product's flagship mode, and the
fewest visible conflicts. **Tanqih** is the better name if the brand should describe the
whole product rather than one mode. **Sabk** is the most distinctive but carries a real
spoken collision in the Gulf market, which is a market this product specifically targets.

## 6. What the user must decide

These are blocked on a decision or a purchase and cannot be completed here.

| # | Decision | Why it is yours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choose the final name** | A brand decision, not a technical one |
| 2 | **Commission a trademark search** in US, UK, EU and UAE, Nice classes 9 and 42 | No register could be queried from this environment, and the result is a legal opinion |

depend on the final name has been completed, and `src/core/constants.js` records
`PUBLIC_NAME_STATUS = 'provisional'` so the state is visible in the code.
