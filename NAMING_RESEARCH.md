# Naming research

**Run 15 of [`EXECUTION_PLAN.md`](EXECUTION_PLAN.md)** · Research carried out 26 September 2026

> **This document is research, not legal clearance.** Nothing here is a trademark opinion,
> a freedom-to-operate opinion, or a guarantee that a name can be used. A qualified
> trademark attorney must clear the chosen name in every market before it is published.
> Availability signals recorded here are point-in-time and can change within hours.

---

## 1. Why the name has to change

`PRODUCT_PLAN_V2.md` and `DEBRIEF_FOR_OPUS.md` already record that **WriteRight** is
heavily used. The problems, restated:

| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| Descriptive and weak | "Write" + "right" is the plainest possible description of a writing corrector. Descriptive marks are hard to register and hard to defend. |
| Crowded | The compound is in wide use across writing tools, education products, and agencies. A web search for the exact phrase returns unrelated commercial uses in the same field. |
| Homophone collision | *WriteRight*, *Write Right*, *Wright*, *Rite* are indistinguishable in speech. A spoken recommendation does not reach the right listing. |
| Untranslatable pun | The wordplay has no Arabic equivalent. Half the product's audience gets a meaningless English compound. |
| No `.com` | `writeright.com` and the obvious variants are long gone. |

**Decision rule carried forward from the user's instruction:** do not publish publicly
under the WriteRight name until these conflicts are addressed.

## 2. Screening criteria

Every candidate was scored against nine criteria before any availability research.

| # | Criterion | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pronounceable by an English speaker on sight | Word of mouth is the main discovery channel for an extension |
| 2 | Pronounceable by an Arabic speaker on sight | Half the product's audience |
| 3 | Uses no phoneme Arabic lacks | Arabic has no **/p/** and no **/v/**; MSA has no hard **/g/** (Egyptian does). A name with them is mispronounced by default |
| 4 | No negative or embarrassing meaning in Arabic | Including near-homophones in Egyptian and Gulf dialects |
| 5 | No negative meaning in English | Including obvious playground distortions |
| 6 | Two syllables or fewer, ≤ 7 letters | Fits a 16-pixel badge and a Chrome Web Store title |
| 7 | Spellable after hearing it once | No silent letters, no ambiguous doubling |
| 8 | Not descriptive of the goods | A descriptive mark is weak; a suggestive or arbitrary one is defensible |
| 9 | Distinct from existing writing tools | Grammarly, Wordtune, QuillBot, ProWritingAid, Hemingway, Writer, Sudowrite, LanguageTool, Typely, Outwrite |

## 5. Shortlist of three

### 1. Saqel  ·  صَقْل

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /ˈsɑː.kel/ · AR *ṣaql* — two syllables, no /p/, /v/ or hard /g/ |
| **Meaning** | To polish, to burnish. In Arabic it is used of refining metal *and* of refining prose |
| **Fit** | The flagship mode is literally called **Polish**. The name and the product's core promise are the same word in two languages |
| **Arabic check** | No negative sense. No unfortunate near-homophone found. The root ص-ق-ل is unambiguous |
| **English check** | Reads cleanly. No distortion found |
| **Evidence for** | No product, company, store listing or extension found under this name |
| **Evidence against** | `saqel.com` is registered and parked for sale, so acquisition would cost money or the product uses `saqel.app` / `getsaqel.com`. GitHub username `@Saqel` is taken by an individual |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### 2. Tanqih  ·  تَنْقِيح

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /tænˈkiː/ or /tanˈkiːh/ · AR *tanqīḥ* |
| **Meaning** | Revision; the emendation of a text. It is the word an Arabic editor uses for the job |
| **Fit** | Names the whole product rather than one mode, which suits a five-mode tool better than Saqel does |
| **Arabic check** | Precise, professional, and slightly formal. No negative sense |
| **English check** | Spellable after hearing it, but the final /ḥ/ will be dropped by English speakers, giving "tan-KEE" |
| **Evidence for** | No software product, company, store listing or repository found. The only results are dictionary entries |
| **Evidence against** | Seven letters and the *qih* ending will be misspelled by English speakers, which hurts search discovery. `tanqih.com` is parked on Afternic |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### 3. Sabk  ·  سَبْك

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronunciation** | EN /sæbk/ · AR *sabk* — one syllable |
| **Meaning** | To cast or forge metal. In classical Arabic rhetoric, سبك is *the cohesion of style* — precisely what an editor works on, paired with حبك, coherence of meaning |
| **Fit** | The most intellectually satisfying of the three: it is the Arabic critical term for well-made prose |
| **Arabic check** | Recognised by educated Arabic speakers; the rhetorical sense is not common knowledge |
| **English check** | Short and memorable, but the final /bk/ cluster does not exist in English and will be said as "sab" or "sabik" |
| **Evidence for** | No writing product found under the name. `usesabk.com` is unregistered |
| **Evidence against** | **Phonetic near-match to "Sabak"**, a live Google Play app from a Kuwaiti logistics company. Different class and different spelling, but the collision is real in speech and in a Gulf market. `sabk.com` has been registered since 2003 |
| **Unverified** | Every trademark register. Social handles were not checked |

### Recommendation

**Saqel** is the strongest of the three on the evidence gathered: the cleanest phonetics
in both languages, the tightest semantic fit with the product's flagship mode, and the
fewest visible conflicts. **Tanqih** is the better name if the brand should describe the
whole product rather than one mode. **Sabk** is the most distinctive but carries a real
spoken collision in the Gulf market, which is a market this product specifically targets.

## 9. `ekteb.ly` review — 27 September 2026

This is a point-in-time follow-up to the earlier shortlist. It does **not** override the
existing provisional-name decision or constitute legal clearance.

### Domain registration: **no current RDAP registration record; final orderability unconfirmed**

- IANA's `.LY` delegation record identifies `rdap.nic.ly` as the authoritative RDAP
  server and the `.LY` domain as Libya's country-code top-level domain:
  <https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/ly.html> (record last updated 1 November 2025).
- Queried the registry RDAP URL directly on 27 September 2026 (Asia/Dubai; the RDAP record's update timestamp was 26 September UTC):
  <https://rdap.nic.ly/domain/ekteb.ly>. It returned HTTP-style RDAP response
  `{"errorCode":404,"title":"No results for query ekteb.ly"}`. This is a name-specific
  registry response, not an empty page or failed fetch, and indicates there was no
  registered domain record at query time.
- Register.ly says its `.LY` availability result can change and the domain is not secured
  until registration completes: <https://register.ly/whois-lookup/>. Its JavaScript-driven
  availability form did not expose an orderability result for this specific name in this
  environment. A missing RDAP object is strong evidence the name is currently
  **unregistered**, but does not guarantee it is not reserved, restricted, premium-priced,
  or accepted by the registrar at checkout.
- The registrar advertises general `.LY` pricing of $75/year (a promotion was shown), not
  a name-specific quote. No registration was attempted or purchased.
- A search surfaced `www.ekteb.li` in third-party infrastructure data. That is the
  different `.li` extension, not `ekteb.ly`; it is not evidence about registration of the
  requested `.LY` name.

**Conclusion:** `ekteb.ly` appears **unregistered in the authoritative registry** as of
27 September 2026 and is plausibly available, but live registrar checkout must confirm
that it can actually be registered and at what price. Do not treat the RDAP result as a
reservation; someone else could register it at any time.

### Product and name conflicts: **material conflict signal**

- Google Play lists an Arabic writing/keyboard app titled **اكتب** (“Write”), published
  by Soda Development. Its package identifier is `com.soda.ekteb`; the listing describes
  Arabic writing and a keyboard. Search results showed 100K+ downloads at the time
  accessed. This is a real product in an adjacent writing category, not merely a generic
  dictionary match: <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soda.ekteb&hl=en_US&gl=US>.
- The same product is listed by Café Bazaar under the `com.soda.ekteb` identifier:
  <https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.soda.ekteb?l=en>.
- Search also found an Egyptian stationery/bookstore business using **Ekteb**:
  <https://www.facebook.com/EktebStore/>; a logo portfolio describes an Arabic site
  called Ekteb, meaning “Write/Type”: <https://logopond.com/geniuslogo/showcase/detail/127900>.
  These additional findings are weaker than the app-store listing and are not trademark
  register results.
- Targeted Chrome Web Store searching did not surface an exact Ekteb extension, but this
  search is not exhaustive and says nothing about trademark rights.

The Google Play result is enough to flag a **material naming collision**, especially for
an English/Arabic writing product addressing Arabic-speaking users. It does not prove
infringement or establish who has superior rights. A trademark search and qualified
advice would still be needed.

### Language and likely confusion

`اكتب` is the masculine singular imperative “write!” (standard Arabic *uktub*). “Ekteb”
is a commonly used informal Latin spelling; an Egyptian Arabic teaching source also gives
*akteb* for the masculine singular imperative. English readers may say “ek-teb” or
“eck-teb,” while Arabic speakers are likely to recognize the intended writing command.
Sources: <https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A8> and
<https://www.ithacaboundlanguages.com/write-imperative-in-egyptian-arabic/>.
The meaning fits the product, but that direct descriptiveness also makes the name less
ownable and increases the chance of confusion with existing Arabic-writing products.
The full address can also be read as “ekteb li” (اكتب لي, “write to me”), a natural phrase
rather than a distinct coined word; `.LY` is Libya's country-code TLD, which can carry a
geographic association as well as work as an English `-ly` domain hack. Sources: [IANA delegation record for .LY](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/ly.html)
and [Arabic examples for “اكتب لي”](https://context.reverso.net/translation/arabic-english/%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A8+%D9%84%D9%8A).

### Decision gate

**Do not adopt `Ekteb` or change public branding yet.** The authoritative RDAP lookup
shows no registration record, but registrar orderability and price still need confirmation.
More importantly, the adjacent Arabic-writing app is a material conflict signal. The
working name remains provisional. No logo, extension UI, mockup, or demo branding has
been changed while this decision is unresolved. Trademark clearance has not been
performed; domain availability, even if confirmed, would not be trademark clearance.

**Sources accessed 27 September 2026.** This is a material conflict finding, not a legal
conclusion. The owner chose to set Ekteb aside and request alternatives; the screened
candidate list, registrar observations, and draft store copy are in
[`BRAND_ALTERNATIVES.md`](BRAND_ALTERNATIVES.md).

### Meaning and audience fit

English/Arabic rewriting and review tools. The initial /spl/ cluster and /p/ are awkward
for many Arabic speakers, which undermines bilingual friendliness.

### Decision gate

  “Nimbly.”

  anti-slop cleaner. It can cover English and Arabic writing modes and future features.
- **No exact-name product surfaced** in the quoted-name and product searches. The search
  did surface other, unrelated uses of “Saffron” (including building-automation AI and
  digital learning), so the compound is not legally cleared and the shared root deserves
  trademark review.
