# Provisional brand alternatives

**Research date: 27 September 2026 (Asia/Dubai).** These are proposals only. None is
selected or legally cleared, and no domain has been purchased. The current extension
branding remains unchanged until the owner chooses a name.

## Availability and conflict screen

Each `.com` below was checked in Namecheap's name-specific registration results on the
research date. Each result displayed the exact domain with an **“Add to cart”** option,
which is a live registrar availability signal. This is not a reservation: availability,
price, premium/reserved status and final eligibility can change before checkout. No
purchase was attempted. Search pages are linked in the table for rechecking.

Targeted exact-name web searches, including Chrome Web Store searches, did not surface an
obvious exact-match writing product for the ten candidates below. Search coverage is not
exhaustive; a negative search is not trademark clearance. Common-law use, social handles,
company registries and trademark registers in the UAE, US, UK, EU and other target markets
have not been comprehensively searched. Ask a trademark professional to clear the final
choice before public release.

## Marketing line and Chrome Web Store description

The descriptions are draft copy, not final store claims. They describe the existing product:
writing tools in English and Arabic, OpenRouter or Google AI Studio with the user's own
key, a side-by-side proposal and explicit accept action, and no product backend. Text the
user submits is processed by the selected provider or configured endpoint; provider terms
and any usage charges apply. “Research” is OpenRouter-only and requires a compatible
model, so it is intentionally not promised in every listing below.

### 1. PhrasePebble

**Marketing line:** Small edits. Smoother sentences.

**Store description:** PhrasePebble helps you polish, soften, or sharpen text where you
write—in English or Arabic. Choose a writing mode, compare the suggested revision with
your original, and accept it only when it feels right. Connect your own OpenRouter or
Google AI Studio key; your text goes to the provider you select, not a PhrasePebble server.

### 2. WordMorrow

**Marketing line:** Make the next message sound like you.

**Store description:** WordMorrow brings practical writing and review tools into your
browser, with support for English and Arabic. Get a proposed rewrite, inspect what
changed, and decide whether to apply it. Bring your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio
key; requests are sent to the provider or endpoint you choose.

### 3. DraftKettle

**Marketing line:** Warm up the draft. Keep your voice.

**Store description:** DraftKettle offers focused ways to polish, rephrase, and review text
without taking the final say away from you. Work in English or Arabic, compare the
proposal with your original, and apply it yourself. Use your own OpenRouter or Google AI
Studio key; provider usage and terms apply.

### 4. EditMirth

**Marketing line:** Clearer writing, with a lighter touch.

**Store description:** EditMirth helps tidy wording, adjust tone, and review reasoning in
the places you type. It supports English and Arabic and shows the proposed changes before
you accept them. Connect your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key; submitted text is
processed by your selected provider or explicit custom endpoint.

### 5. WordSaffron

**Marketing line:** A pinch of clarity. Still your words.

**Store description:** WordSaffron offers English and Arabic writing modes for polishing,
tone changes, and review. See the suggested version beside your text, then choose whether
to apply it. Use your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key. The extension has no product
backend; your selected provider processes the text you submit.

### 6. DraftBreeze

**Marketing line:** Let the next draft breathe.

**Store description:** DraftBreeze gives you an on-demand writing companion for English
and Arabic. Choose a mode, review a side-by-side rewrite, and accept only the changes you
want. Connect your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key; requests go to the provider or
endpoint you configure, under that service's terms.

### 7. TextApricot

**Marketing line:** Freshen the text. Keep the meaning.

**Store description:** TextApricot helps refine messages, emails, and other text wherever
you write. Use English or Arabic modes, inspect the proposed changes, and decide before
anything is applied. It uses your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key and sends
submitted text only to the provider or custom endpoint you select.

### 8. PhraseMoss

**Marketing line:** Grounded words. No extra flourish.

**Store description:** PhraseMoss helps you polish, rephrase, and review English or Arabic
text while keeping you in control. Compare the suggestion with your original before you
accept it. Connect your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key; the selected provider
processes each request, and its own terms and charges apply.

### 9. DraftDandelion

**Marketing line:** Shape the draft you already wrote.

**Store description:** DraftDandelion brings English and Arabic writing modes to the text
fields you use every day. Ask for a rewrite or review, inspect the proposed result, and
apply it only if you choose. Bring your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key; requests
are routed to your selected provider or configured endpoint.

### 10. PhraseMallow

**Marketing line:** A softer landing for every sentence.

**Store description:** PhraseMallow helps make everyday writing clearer and more
considerate in English or Arabic. Choose a mode, compare the proposed edit with your
original, and accept it yourself. Use your own OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key; your
request is processed by the provider you choose, not by a PhraseMallow backend.

## Early creative favourites (not a decision)

- **PhraseMallow** — warm and distinctive, with a clear visual world.
- **WordSaffron** — memorable and regionally resonant without pretending to be an Arabic
  word.
- **DraftKettle** — the most playful; best if the desired voice is intentionally quirky.

All are just naming concepts. A final pick should get a fresh domain check at checkout and
professional trademark screening before it becomes the public brand.

## Follow-up: custom name `Deslopify`

The custom name suggested after the list was screened on 27 September 2026. It is **not**
recommended as a default: `deslopify.com` is registered (RDAP registration date 21
December 2024), a Firefox extension named **Deslopify** already addresses intrusive AI
features in the browser, and a separate Deslopify tool targets AI code quality. See §11
of [`NAMING_RESEARCH.md`](NAMING_RESEARCH.md) for sources. No branding has been applied.
