## Long description — listing draft

```text
A little more clarity, while keeping the words yours.

WordSaffron brings writing and review tools into the text fields where you work. Choose a mode, inspect the proposed change, and apply it only if you want to.

FIVE MODES FOR TEXT YOU ALREADY WROTE
• Polish — same voice, cleaner writing
• Casual — natural and easygoing
• Polite — respectful without weakening the message
• Professional & Firm — direct, confident, and business-ready
• Technical Review — check the reasoning, then improve the response

ENGLISH AND ARABIC
British English is the default. Arabic behavior depends on the selected mode: Modern Standard Arabic for formal/professional modes and natural Egyptian Arabic for Casual. Mixed Arabic, English, URLs, and Arabic-Indic digits are handled with direction-aware layout.

KEEP CONTROL OF THE EDIT
Suggestions are shown for review; WordSaffron does not apply a rewrite without your action. It checks that important details from your original—such as numbers, names, dates, URLs, and identifiers—survive the rewrite. AI output can still be wrong, so review it before using it.

CHOOSE YOUR PROVIDER
Use your own supported OpenRouter or Google AI Studio key, or the explicit endpoint configured in settings. Your key is stored in local Chrome extension storage and is not synced through Chrome Sync. When you request a rewrite or review, the text you submit is sent to the selected provider/endpoint and is handled under its terms. Provider usage charges may apply.

TECHNICAL REVIEW
Logic-only review does not use web research and cannot verify outside facts. Researched Technical Review is available through OpenRouter only and uses its web-search tool; it may incur provider charges. Google AI Studio supports logic-only review, not researched review.

YOUR CONTROLS
No WordSaffron backend, account, analytics, or telemetry. Requests happen when you ask for them. Turn the extension off globally, for a site, or for a tab. Nearby conversation context is off by default and requires both a global setting and per-site permission, with the exact text shown before sending.

AI output can be wrong. Review every suggestion before using it.
```

## FAQ draft

**Does WordSaffron include AI usage?**  
No. You provide a supported API key, and your selected provider may charge for requests. If a one-time Pro purchase is introduced, that purchase will cover the extension's stated Pro features—not provider usage—unless the terms explicitly change.

**Does it send every page I visit?**  
No. A request is made when you trigger a rewrite or review. Nearby conversation context is optional, off by default, and requires explicit setup for each supported site.

**Can I trust every suggestion?**  
No. AI output can be wrong. Review suggestions before using them.

**Can I use researched review with Google AI Studio?**  
No. Researched Technical Review is OpenRouter-only. Google AI Studio supports logic-only review and the writing tools.
